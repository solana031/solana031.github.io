About ReposNews

Tools worth knowing. Context to make better choices.

ReposNews is an independent publication about software, repositories and developer tools. We explain interesting projects through clear analysis, practical guides and links to original sources.

Our approach

We focus on usefulness, getting started and each tool’s limitations. We check official documentation and distinguish documented features from hands-on testing.

Who is behind it

ReposNews is edited from Spain by its publisher. The publisher’s full details are available in the legal notice and you can write to zaragente031@gmail.com.

What we publish

Guides in Spanish and English about open-source projects and software tools: what problem they solve, how they work, what you need to start, how to install and use them step by step, common errors, limitations, alternatives and our assessment of who they are worth it for.

How each guide is made

  1. We select active projects we see in the community (trending repositories and tools shared on social media) and discard those without verifiable documentation or an official source.
  2. We read the official repository, its documentation and its license. Installation commands are copied from the official documentation; we never invent steps.
  3. We draft with the help of artificial intelligence tools and apply an independent review that checks every claim and command against the official sources before publishing.
  4. Every guide shows when its sources were checked and links to them so you can verify them.

Updates and corrections

We periodically review published guides: when a project changes its version, license or installation method, we update the article and its date. If you find an error, write to us with the guide’s link and we will correct it.

Editorial independence

We do not represent the projects featured in our guides and are not paid to include them. Software, trademarks and licenses belong to their authors. If we show advertising in the future, it will be clearly separated from editorial content and will not influence our assessments.
