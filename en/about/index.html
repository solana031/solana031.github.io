About ReposNews

ReposNews covers repositories and developer tools: the problems they solve, how to get started and what to check before adoption.

Editorial approach

Projects are selected for usefulness and available evidence. We check documentation, licenses and releases. Star counts do not measure recent popularity; documented commands are distinguished from installations actually tested. Spanish and English versions are reviewed together.

Projects belong to their authors. ReposNews is not GitHub and does not represent maintainers. Analysis is editorial and independent; names and trademarks belong to their owners and original licenses take precedence over our descriptions. We publish original analysis and link to source material.
