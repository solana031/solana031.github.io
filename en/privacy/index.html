Instagram data

ZAR ReposNews Integration processes connected-account data and content from the authorized sender to prepare guides: account and message identifiers, dates, text, links and attachments. Accessible media may be analysed with Google Gemini. Private messages are not published and these data are not sold.

Authorized access covers basic account information, messages and content publishing. Editorial data follows the retention criteria stated in this policy.

Revoke access or delete data

You can revoke access to ZAR ReposNews Integration-IG under Apps and websites in Instagram settings. To request deletion of associated data, email zaragente031@gmail.com with the subject “Delete Instagram data” and your username. Revoking access does not itself delete previously stored data.

Cookies and use of data for personalization will require consent where applicable. Providers and purposes will be identified in the certified consent manager before activation.

You will be able to consent, reject or manage options and withdraw consent through privacy options. You can also read how Google uses information from partner sites and manage personalization in My Ad Center.

Your rights

Where applicable, you may request access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and data portability. You may withdraw consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of earlier processing. Email zaragente031@gmail.com.
