ZAR Instagram integration

ZAR ReposNews Integration connects the professional account @zaragente031 through OAuth, requesting only instagram_business_basic and instagram_business_manage_messages. It verifies the account ID and username, reads conversation metadata to corroborate the authorized sender, and receives message events.

The editorial workflow processes only content intended for ReposNews from a sender previously authorized by Instagram ID. It may retain message ID, sender, date, editorial text, shared links and attachment metadata. Accessible media may be analysed by the configured AI provider (currently Gemini). Discovery content is checked against official technical sources.

OAuth tokens and temporary media references are encrypted on the server. Temporary references are deleted after the analysis attempt. Drafts and sources follow the retention information approved by the owner. Event diagnostics retain field-presence flags, without text or IDs. We do not sell these data or publish private messages. Article publication requires human confirmation.

Revoke access and request deletion

Revoke access in Instagram settings under Apps and websites, selecting ZAR ReposNews Integration-IG. Revocation stops further API queries but does not automatically remove existing drafts.

To request deletion of tokens, identifiers, editorial events or drafts associated with your account, email zaragente031@gmail.com with subject “Delete Instagram data”. Include your Instagram username and the affected article or message if relevant. Never send passwords, codes, tokens or identity documents. Requests are handled through this contact; this page does not perform automatic deletion.

Providers, retention and rights

Infrastructure uses Railway and IONOS DNS; contact messages are received in Google Gmail. Processing and retention arrangements reflect the information approved by the owner. Use the privacy contact for access, correction, deletion or other applicable rights requests.

Analytics and advertising are inactive. Adding measurement or ads requires updating this information and configuring the applicable consent mechanism.
