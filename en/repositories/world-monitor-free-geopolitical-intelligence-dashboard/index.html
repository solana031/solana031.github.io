Purpose and Overview

World Monitor is described in its official documentation as a real-time geopolitical intelligence dashboard built for analysts, journalists, traders, and builders who require live geopolitical signals. According to the provider, the free service streams 469 news and OSINT feeds from 770 attributed providers onto a single interactive world map featuring 58 map layer types, covering domains such as geopolitics, markets, shipping, aviation, and cyber.

Requirements and Documented Setup

According to the provider's sources, the dashboard is web-optimized and accessible directly without mandatory signups. Official documentation also outlines a REST API and an MCP (Model Context Protocol) server, with developer integration paths referenced via npm, PyPI, Go, and RubyGems.

Usage and Capabilities

Official documentation details structured evidence trails designed for specific analytical workflows:

1. **Verify a breaking event**: Check crisis trackers and dated metrics.

2. **Check a chokepoint**: Inspect disruption status and vessel flows across 13 maritime passages.

3. **Explain a country-risk move**: Trace political, security, and economic signals across 170 countries.

Provider Claims vs Source Review

* **Provider Claims**: The creator states that the platform serves over 2 million users and operates as an open-source project under the AGPL-3.0 license with professional tiers.

* **Source Review**: Examination of public documentation confirms references to 58 map layers, OSINT news feeds, and developer integration capabilities via API and MCP servers.

Advantages and Limitations

* **Advantages**: Web-based access described as free without signup, extensive cartographic layers, and documented support for developers and AI agents.

* **Limitations**: Advanced analytical modules such as the physical premium divergence index and supply-vulnerability rankings are designated as PRO features.

Alternatives and Editorial Comparison

Unlike traditional proprietary platforms, the project's documentation highlights its open-source AGPL-3.0 license and free baseline tier for geopolitical data visualization.

License and Official Pricing

The core service is stated to be free ($0) and open-source under AGPL-3.0. Advanced PRO features and commercial usage details are governed by the provider's official terms.
