Overview and Purpose

Granola is an AI-driven notepad designed for individuals handling back-to-back meetings. Its primary core purpose is to log conversation context, produce meeting transcriptions, capture action items, and build a continuous memory of prior discussions without requiring an external meeting bot to join the call.

Requirements and Platform Support

Based on official documentation and service metadata, Granola provides native client availability for:

- **Desktop:** macOS and Windows.

- **Mobile:** iOS and Android.

Because the service processes computer audio directly from the host device, it works with Zoom, Google Meet, Microsoft Teams, and any other conferencing or softphone software.

How Granola Works

Granola structures meeting workflow across three distinct operational stages:

1. **Before the Meeting:** Integrates with the user's calendar to compile a pre-meeting Brief detailing participants, previous conversation points, and critical agenda items.

2. **During the Meeting:** Processes system audio while allowing the user to take lightweight or thorough manual notes. Granola combines manual input with call context to enhance the final notes.

3. **After the Meeting:** Automatically compiles summary notes, action items, follow-up email drafts, and preliminary project plans upon call completion.

It also incorporates mobile voice typing and a built-in AI chat interface capable of querying stored meeting context.

Advantages and Limitations

### Claimed Advantages

- **No Meeting Bot:** Operates via computer audio capture rather than sending a visible third-party bot into video rooms.

- **Private by Default:** Note output remains private unless explicitly shared by the user.

- **Multi-Platform:** Supports desktop, mobile calls on the go, and calendar-driven briefing.

### Limitations

- **Proprietary Service:** As a commercial software product, self-hosted or open-source local deployments are not documented.

- **System Prerequisites:** Exact hardware specifications, CPU usage limits, and bandwidth requirements are not detailed in the source material.

Comparison and Alternatives

Unlike traditional AI meeting assistants that connect as extra participants inside video call software, Granola operates locally via system audio capture. This architecture distinguishes it from standard bot-based meeting recorders by preserving room privacy while still offering context-aware transcription and action item extraction.
