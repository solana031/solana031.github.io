Purpose

**Stickman Video Director** is an AI stickman animation style library and video prompt generator. Its primary purpose is to turn any idea or topic into a high-quality stickman video with a stable visual style, making it ideal for YouTube knowledge explanations, TikTok content, professional motivation, educational science popularization, and fast-paced emotional storytelling.

Requirements and Setup

Designed as an AI skill, the package requires no manual API keys or MCP configuration according to the official documentation. To install the skill, provide the following command to your supported AI assistant:

帮我安装这个 Skill：
https://github.com/kaomei/stickman-video-director

Usage

Once installed, the documented workflow follows these steps:

1. **Provide a Topic**: Send your topic, copywriting, or article directly to the AI.

2. **Review the Director's Proposal**: The skill outputs a structural proposal covering narrative breakdown, segmented voiceover, visual actions, camera movements, and audio design.

3. **Confirm and Modify**: Adjust any unsatisfactory parts of the proposal before obtaining the final prompts.

4. **Generate and Edit**: Submit the generated prompts sequentially into video platforms like Google Flow, then import the clips into external editors to finalize subtitles and assembly.

Example usage prompt:

帮我做一个《人类的第一份工作是怎么来的？》的火柴人视频。

Documented Styles

The repository organizes several distinct visual styles:

- **Style 1 (Basic Black & White)**: Minimal lines for clear conceptual explanations.

- **Style 2 (Modern Tech & Story)**: Consistent character designs featuring a red hat and yellow shirt, split into 2A (tech minimalism) and 2B (colored storytelling).

- **Style 3 (Prehistoric / Dark Humor)**: Rough hand-drawn aesthetics for satirical workplace shorts and historical anecdotes.

- **Style 4 (Cozy Healing Stories)**: Everyday colored environments focused on personal reflection and relatable scenarios.

Limitations

The skill handles proposals and prompt generation only; actual video rendering relies on third-party generative tools (such as Google Flow), and generation costs depend entirely on external platform pricing.
