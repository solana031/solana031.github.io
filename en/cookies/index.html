Cookies

Current use

reposnews_lang remembers the language manually selected: ES or EN. It is a first-party preference cookie lasting up to 365 days, with Path=/, SameSite=Lax and Secure. It is created when the selector is used. Analytics, AdSense and CMP scripts are not currently loaded.

Control and future changes

Cookies can be removed or blocked in your browser. Before serving ads in regions requiring it, a Google-certified CMP and a clear choice must be configured; advertising and measurement must not be enabled by default.
