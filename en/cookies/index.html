Cookie policy

A language preference. No advertising or analytics cookies.

Cookies are small files stored by your browser. ReposNews uses the following first-party cookie only when you select a language:

Name
reposnews_lang
Purpose
Remember your choice of Spanish or English.
Provider
ReposNews
Duration
Up to 365 days.

This preference follows your explicit choice and does not require additional consent when used solely to remember your language.

How to manage it

You can change the language using the website selector and delete or block this cookie in your browser settings. Deleting it removes your saved preference.

Cookie and technology categories

  • Necessary: site delivery and security functions. They are not used for advertising.
  • Preferences: the language cookie described above, tied to your explicit selection.
  • Analytics: no audience analytics is enabled.
  • Advertising and personalization: Google AdSense may use cookies and identifiers for ads and measurement if this service is enabled. They are currently disabled.
  • Third parties: providers and their purposes will be shown in the certified CMP before optional technologies are enabled.

Privacy options

No advertising or optional analytics is loaded at present, so there is no active advertising consent to manage. Accepting advertising cookies is not required to read ReposNews. Before enabling them, a certified consent platform will offer consent, rejection and preference management without visually favoring acceptance. You will be able to withdraw consent through this entry.

You can also manage Google personalized ads in My Ad Center. These settings do not replace consent required on this website.
